Privacy policy

Gmail Connector · last updated 8 November 2026

Who uses it

Gmail Connector is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, signed in with the owner's own Google accounts.

What it can access

  • Email, conversations and labels (read only), to search and read email when the owner asks.
  • Drafts, to save drafts the owner asks for. It never sends email.
  • The account's email address, to tell the accounts apart.

How email is used

Email is only fetched when the owner asks for it. It's shown to the owner in a conversation with Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, under the owner's own Anthropic account. It isn't used for anything else.

Where data is kept

Gmail Connector comes in two versions, and only the owner uses either of them.

The local version runs on the owner's computer. Its Google sign-in keys stay on that computer.

The hosted version runs on Cloudflare Workers, so the owner can use it from Claude on the web and on a phone. Its Google sign-in keys are stored on Cloudflare, encrypted. Only the owner's Google account can sign in to it.

Neither version keeps copies of email. Email passes through when the owner asks for it, and isn't stored.

Sharing

Gmail Connector doesn't sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and it doesn't share it with anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in Google Account settings, under Third-party apps and services.
