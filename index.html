Gmail Connector

Gmail Connector is a personal tool. It lets Claude, an AI assistant, search, read and draft email across its owner's Gmail accounts, without switching between them.

It runs on the owner's own computer, and as a private copy on Cloudflare so the owner can use it from the web and a phone. It isn't a public service, and nobody else uses it.

It can read email and save drafts. It can't send, delete or archive anything. The owner checks and sends every draft in Gmail.
